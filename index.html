About these stories

All 42 expanded stories are included in this file and can be read without an internet connection. Every story contains at least 900 words. Supporting characters, dialogue, and personal scenes are fictional, including dialogue given to historical figures. These are companion scenes, not the original book or Bible quotations.

The later doctrinal and future scenes follow the book’s Seventh-day Adventist interpretation. The collection’s religious perspective is distinct from a claim that these future events are established facts or beliefs shared by all Christians.

The stories follow the 42-chapter sequence of the original book.
